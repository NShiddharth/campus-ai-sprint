# 3-Minute Video Presentation Script: Campus AI Sprint Growth Engine

**Speaker Tone**: Crisp, energetic, pragmatic, and growth-oriented. Speak like an engineer pitching to a VP of Growth.

---

### [0:00 – 0:20] The Problem & The Target

> *"Hi everyone. For Round 1 of the NxtWave Growth Challenge, the objective was clear: acquire 500 final-year engineering students for a free 60-minute workshop called 'Build Your First AI Project' — with a ₹2,000 budget in 7 days.*
>
> *At ₹4 per registration, paid performance ads simply will not work. To hit 500 students, the growth mechanism cannot just be marketing spend — the product itself must be an organic viral growth engine."*

---

### [0:20 – 0:50] The Student Insight: Why Engineering Students Act

> *"Before writing any code, I analyzed why a final-year engineering student in India actually registers.*
>
> *Students don't want another theoretical lecture on machine learning algorithms. Placements are right around the corner, and their biggest anxiety is having an empty resume or failing technical interviews.*
>
> *Our core message addresses that exact pain point:*
>
> **'Go from I want an AI project to a working project you can explain in your next interview — in 60 minutes, for free.'**
>
> *They get a GitHub repository, 3 resume bullet points, and the exact talking points recruiters look for."*

---

### [0:50 – 1:20] The Growth Strategy: Multi-Sided Viral Loop

> *"To reach 500 registrations on ₹2,000, I designed a 4-channel focused campaign anchored by a built-in referral loop:*
>
> 1. *A student registers in 30 seconds.*
> 2. *They instantly receive a unique referral link and dashboard.*
> 3. *They share it with their department WhatsApp group to boost their college on the Campus Leaderboard.*
> 4. *Referring 3 classmates unlocks an exclusive AI Project Interview Prep Toolkit.*
>
> *By combining inter-college competition with milestone incentives, our organic referral coefficient $K$ reaches 0.4, generating over 140 peer sign-ups on autopilot."*

---

### [1:20 – 2:20] Live Product Walkthrough

*(Screen Share Demonstration)*

> *"Let me show you the working application.*
>
> *Here on the landing page, students see our live progress widget showing 370 of 500 seats filled in real time from our backend. Below, an interactive preview shows the exact project they will build — an ATS Resume Matcher and Interview Pitch Generator with real code snippets.*
>
> *When a student registers, notice how clean the form is. It captures their college, graduation year, and detects referral codes automatically. Duplicate emails and numbers are prevented at the database level.*
>
> *Upon registering, watch the confetti burst. The student lands on their personal Referral Dashboard. They see their college's current rank, their referral count, and one-click buttons to share pre-filled messages directly to WhatsApp and LinkedIn.*
>
> *Next, our Campus Leaderboard displays live standings across institutions like IIT Madras, DTU, and BITS Pilani, with individual student names privacy-masked.*
>
> *Finally, let's open the Admin Growth Portal. Organizers see the real-time target status, the 5-step conversion funnel from Landing View to Referral Conversion, acquisition channels, daily trends, and proposed A/B growth experiments — all computed directly from SQL database records."*

---

### [2:20 – 2:45] Why This Asset Scales the Campaign

> *"Why does this asset scale?*
>
> *Because it solves student acquisition through product mechanics rather than manual outreach. The ₹2,000 budget isn't wasted on untargeted ads; ₹700 is allocated to seed active student WhatsApp placement channels, ₹500 rewards top campus ambassadors, and ₹800 is reserved to double-down on the highest-converting college communities identified by Day 4."*

---

### [2:45 – 3:00] What I Rejected, Learned & What’s Next

> *"I intentionally rejected building a static landing page because it fails to generate viral momentum. I also rejected 18 unverified marketing channels to focus strictly on where Indian engineering students actually hang out.*
>
> *If I had one more week, I would integrate automated WhatsApp reminder webhooks 1 hour before the workshop to maximize the registration-to-attendance rate.*
>
> *Thank you, and I look forward to contributing to NxtWave's growth team."*
